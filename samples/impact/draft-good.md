# Spring 2026 Reading Programs: Impact Summary (synthetic)

We served 20 people this spring. Of them, 80.0% (16 of 20) completed their program; in Tutoring, 83.3% (10 of 12) completed.

Among the 16 participants with both a starting and an ending score, the average score rose from 44.69 to 55.31, a change of 10.63 points. 4 participants had no ending score and are not included. This change is associated with the program; without a comparison group we can't say the program caused it.

Tutoring had 12 enrollments. The average starting score across all participants was 44.0 (median 45).
